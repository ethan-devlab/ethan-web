## Users

Three homepage directions are under review: technical partners who need evidence of engineering judgment; hiring managers who need a clear view of skills and experience; creative peers who explore original work and personal perspective. Visitors use both phones and desktop screens. The owner will select the primary audience after comparing working prototypes.

## Product Purpose

Present Ethan Chan's engineering work, learning, writing, and photography. Help visitors understand his work and reach the relevant project, resume, or contact channel. Preserve the existing bilingual routes and all current content.

## Brand Personality

Precise, curious, grounded. Show a real person who builds software and observes the world. The three directions express this through construction, a professional profile, and photographic observation.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic agency templates, repeated icon cards, invented results, gradient text, decorative glass panels, and effects that obstruct reading or navigation. Awards are a craft reference, not a claim of recognition.

## Design Principles

- Use real work and original images as evidence.
- Give each audience a clear route into the existing content.
- Make each direction distinct in composition and narrative, not just color.
- Keep text, links, and controls usable without animation.
- Compare working homepages before choosing a site-wide direction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard use, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, touch controls, English, Traditional Chinese, and both themes. Verify the prototypes in a real browser. Automated checks alone do not establish compliance.
